  1. v1.12026-10-22

    Scope tag for products. While reviewing the execution epic, the owner spotted two tickets from other work (an app UI rework and uptime monitoring). Fix: they were removed from the epic, the phases were renumbered (19 sub-tickets), and a kube-poc tag now goes on every related ticket. All relevant bots were told. Lesson: tag product scope from day one, so an AI-built roadmap can't pull in tickets that only look related. Tracker limitation: the ClickUp API can't clear a parent, so moving those two tickets out of the epic is a manual step in the app.

    Living-guide cadence. Every significant process change the bots report gets a changelog entry. The guide goes public one week after v1.0 and is refreshed every three months. It also works as a rulebook: when a new ask conflicts with an earlier rule, the bots point to the chapter and ask before acting.
